Rules

  • What animates: state changes (a cross-fade, a colour change, a strike-through), arrivals (rows, cards, notes), layout moves (FLIP), progress (shimmer, pulse, ring, line fill) and counts (a digit tick).
  • What doesn't: the focus ring, typed text and the scroll jump to the newest message. An amount never morphs from one number to another: the old one strikes through and the new one arrives, so both stay readable. Nothing loops except an indicator of work in progress, and it stops when the work stops.
  • Enter vs exit: enters take 220–400ms on ease-out or settle, with at most 10px of travel and 4px of blur. Exits take about 60% as long (130–250ms) on ease-exit, fade with at most 6px of travel, and stagger only in reverse at 40ms.
  • Stagger limits: 45–80ms apart, at most 6 items (the 7th onwards arrive with the 6th), and 400ms in total. Streamed words keep their own pace (30ms per word), which isn't a stagger.
  • Interruptible: state changes are CSS transitions, so they retarget from wherever they are. A scripted sequence is cancelled when it is replayed or its state changes. A click never waits for an animation, and nothing queues.
  • Reduced motion (the OS setting or the toggle above): travel, scale, blur and height snap. Anything that would move cross-fades over --dur-fade instead. Loops stop: the spinner becomes a still ring beside its word, and shimmer becomes plain text. Sequences keep their order and timing, so progress is still visible. There is never zero feedback.

Trace rows enter like agents-ui trace-waterfall (--dur-row, 45ms apart), their bars grow from the start time, and the running bar sweeps every 1600ms. Memory rows stagger in like collapsible items.

focus
Trace and Memory are toggle buttons (aria-pressed). Opening a rail doesn't move focus.
label
The rails are complementary regions named "Trace" and "Memory".
contrast
The rail is surface-card against the canvas chat, with an edge hairline.
motion
Under reduced motion, the layout snaps and the rail cross-fades.
