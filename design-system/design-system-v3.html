v3: rebased on Lime

v2 was Errand's own dark, dense system. The owner reviewed it and picked Lime instead, so v3 takes Lime's foundations and components as they are and rebuilds each Errand pattern from them. Errand's product rules come along unchanged.

What changed from v2, and why

  • Foundations are Lime's. Colour, type, spacing, radius, elevation and motion tokens are copied verbatim from Lime's tokens.css, with its dark theme and the glow classes. Every v2 token is dropped or renamed; the token mapping lists each one.
  • Light by default. The page opens light whatever the OS prefers, frames, overlays and chrome alike. Dark is only behind the toggle and is not remembered.
  • Photos where they help someone choose. Provider rows, service tiles, home suggestions and the "Which AC service?" options carry a real photo; the approval card and receipt carry a small provider thumbnail. Status, receipts beyond a thumbnail and decoration stay photo-free. See Images and Credits.
  • One typeface. Geist and Geist Mono give way to Hanken Grotesk. Figures use tabular numerals in the same face; mono is kept for codes only (booking IDs, check-in codes, tool names in the operator console), as Lime asks.
  • One lime action per view. The primary was an ink fill in v2. It is now Lime's glowing primary, and each framed screen on this page holds at most one. Approve is that one action whenever an approval card shows.
  • Patterns rebuilt from Lime components. The approval card is ApprovalRequest, the tracker is ToolCall rows under a Status, the receipt is ActionReceipt, suggestions are FollowUps and Chip, provider cards are a List, the ask form is Field, and states use Empty, ErrorState and Skeleton.
  • No eyebrows or small capitals. v2's uppercase specimen labels, uppercase table headers and letter spacing are gone. Sections are named by an h2 at text-lg semibold, or not at all.
  • No decoration. v2's photo gradients, emoji service marks, status glyphs and "new" and "fixed" tags are gone. Status is a dot or spinner beside a word; icons come from one line set.
  • No Undo. Errand cannot reverse a booking, so the receipt offers Reschedule and Cancel booking, and the memory line reads "Memory updated · View". v2's memory note had an Undo the backend does not support.
  • Folded together. The context echo became the request chips, the slot picker became the day and time chips of the ask form, agent progress became ToolCall rows, and visit day and after service sit with bookings. The six-frame journey strip and the "Don't" specimens are gone, since each pattern is now a full view.

Motion removed because Lime rejects it

  • Overshoot curve --ease-pop (spring): the done disc pop, badge pop, star pop and difference-chip pop.
  • Extra curves --ease-snap, --ease-settle, --ease-sheet and --ease-exit. v3 uses --ease-out to arrive and --ease-in to leave.
  • v2's per-word streaming fade (--dur-word) and its fixed word delay. v3 streams answers with a paced reveal instead, a deliberate bend listed below.
  • Text shimmer on running labels (--dur-shimmer).
  • Skeleton sweep (--dur-skeleton, a loop). Skeletons are still.
  • Active-step pulse (--dur-pulse, a loop), the trace waterfall's running-bar sweep (a loop) and the bell badge's blur pulse.
  • Scroll reveal: v2 played every demo when it scrolled into view. v3 shows each demo in its settled state and plays it on Replay or on a press.
  • Blur on arrivals (outside the streamed words), the failure shake, the warn-tint flash on changed values, the check stroke draw, the strike-through draw and the per-digit tick. v3 moves only opacity, translate and scale, plus height or width on a collapsing panel.
  • The 150ms reduced-motion cross-fade (--dur-fade) and v2's still spinner. Reduced motion now zeroes every duration, and the spinner keeps turning because it is the one sign that work is running.
  • "Approving" as a label swap. Approve keeps its label and turns its spinner.

Lime rules we bent, and why

  • Answer text streams in (anti-slop-no-stream-effects). The owner asked for super smooth answer streaming, so this is a deliberate bend with the owner as the reason. A frame-paced reveal speeds up as the backlog grows so it never trails the model by more than --dur-stream-lag, and each new word blurs in over --dur-stream-word. Only new words animate, there is no caret and no shimmer, and the words unwrap to plain text when the answer ends. See Streaming answers.
  • Receipts without Undo (global rule lime-ask-before-money). Errand cannot reverse a booking. We use Lime's own edge case for this: leave Undo out, say why in the receipt, and offer Reschedule and Cancel booking.
  • The amount in the Approve label (button-amount-in-label, a should). Errand's rule is that the commit button says what it spends: "Approve ₹899", "Approve move", "Approve free cancellation". The Amount pill still sits in the card header.
  • Three Errand tokens (never invent a token). --errand-ok-fill, --errand-on-ok-fill and --errand-inferred-edge alias Lime roles and add no new value. Lime has no mark for a provider-confirmed booking and no edge for a guess.
  • Errand's status words (status-one-map). There is still one map with five states, but its words are Errand's (Booked, Checking with provider, Not booked, Cancelled) instead of Lime's payment words (Paid, Processing, Declined, Canceled). Spelling is Indian English.
  • Rupees, whole. formatAmount uses en-IN and INR and drops paise on whole amounts, so ₹1,299 and ₹1,29,900; Lime defaults to en-US dollars with cents.
  • Marks cross-fade (tool-call and status say a mark swaps at once). The owner wants every state change animated, so marks and labels cross-fade over --dur-fast, with no layout shift; reduced motion zeroes it.
  • A stale state on ApprovalRequest. Lime's card has waiting, approved and declined. Errand voids a card when the price, slot or provider changes, so the stale card shows why in a warning line and a fresh card takes the glow.
  • No available on approvals. Errand does not hold the person's balance, so Approve never turns off for funds.
  • Overlays contained in their view. Sheets, dialogs and toasts open inside their frame so each demo stays on its own screen; in the app they are fixed to the window.
  • No glow in the operator console (anti-slop-keep-one-glow). It is an internal tool with no next step for the person.
  • One glow per view, not per page. This page is a catalogue of screens. Each frame is a view with at most one lime action, and the page chrome has none.
  • Two dark danger values patched (tokens verbatim). Lime's dark --danger-hover and pressed danger glow top give 4.06:1 and 4.26:1 with --on-danger, under AA. Errand points each at a Lime value that passes, in its own block after the verbatim source, and the contrast check proves it.
  • Icons drawn by hand. The page cannot load @carbon/icons, so the glyphs are line stand-ins drawn to the names Lime exports.

Foundations

Every value below is read at run time from Lime's tokens in this file, so the tables cannot drift from the source. Switch the theme at the top to see the dark roles applied; both columns always show both themes.

Colour roles

SwatchTokenRoleLightDark

Lime's glow tokens (--glow-accent-*, --glow-danger-*, --glow-success-*) are read only through the glow classes, never in a component. The three --errand-* rows are Errand's only additions; each aliases a Lime role.

Glow

The one gradient in Lime: a lit lime body on the primary action, a red one on a destructive confirm, and one featured card. It powers up on hover and darkens on press. Disabled has no glow.

Type

Hanken Grotesk at seven sizes and three weights: 600 for headings, 500 for controls and labels, 400 for the rest. There is no 700. Only text-xl and up take tight tracking. Figures that change or line up are tabular.

Spacing and control height

A 4px scale. Related things sit 8px apart, groups 24px or more. Controls are 32, 40 or 48px tall, and side-by-side controls share a height.

Radius

Controls are pills, fields are 12px, panels 16px, sheets and dialogs 24px. An element inside a rounded panel takes a smaller radius.

Elevation

Grey steps for what sits on the page, and a warm low shadow only for what floats and can be dismissed. Dark adds a hairline ring to menus and overlays.

Motion

Short, ease-out to arrive and ease-in to leave, and only when something changes. Popups start at 0.97 and pressed controls go to 0.98. The spinner is the only loop. Reduced motion zeroes every duration but the spinner and skips the press scale; the state still changes, focus still moves and live regions still speak.

Iconography

One set of simple line icons in currentColor at 16, 20 and 24. Quiet icons use --fg-3; on white an accent icon uses --accent-line, never the lime. An icon never carries a status alone.

Images

Use a photo when it helps someone choose: between providers, between services, between kinds of one service. A provider thumbnail may sit on an approval card or a receipt so the person recognises who is coming. Never use a photo for status, decoration or a background, and never set text on a photo; a label sits below it on a solid surface.

  • Aspect ratios. 1:1 for a provider or team (40px on cards and receipts, 56px in lists, 24px inside a suggestion); 4:3 for a service tile or an option card (120px wide in a tile row, a third of the view in an option grid); 16:9 for a hero or a job photo.
  • Files. Real photos under the Unsplash License, resized to 160px square or 320 by 240 and compressed to under 32 KB each, inlined so the page stays one file.
  • Corners. Concentric with the container: a radius equal to the container's radius minus its padding, and never under --radius-sm. A photo flush with a card's edge takes the card's --radius-panel by clipping; one inside 16px padding takes --radius-sm; a standalone tile takes --radius-field.
  • Loading. A still --surface-2 skeleton holds the exact size, and the photo fades in over --dur-base on --ease-out. Nothing moves when it lands.
  • Failure. The provider's initials, or the service's line icon, on --surface, at the same size.
  • People. Indian home-service workers in their own setting, or neutral tool and job photos. No stock-model portraits. Every photo has alt text that says what it shows.

Home and the composer

Home greets the person, offers the composer and three requests they could have typed, and lists what is coming up. Sending a request turns home into the conversation, and the agent's steps show as quiet ToolCall rows, never as "Thinking".

Built from ChatInput, FollowUps, Message, ToolCall, List and Status. Send is the only lime, and it lights only once there is text. The rows sit in a polite live region.

Motion A pressed suggestion scales to 0.98 and sends as written. The row leaves on --ease-in over --dur-fast, home's lists collapse, and the bubble and each step arrive with a 4px rise over --dur-base. A step's mark and label cross-fade from running to done.

Streaming answers

Models send text in bursts of uneven size with uneven gaps. Errand buffers what arrives and reveals it at a time-based rate on every frame. The rate rises with the backlog, so the reveal never trails the model by more than --dur-stream-lag and never stalls on a burst. When the model ends, the rest flushes quickly but still word by word.

Reveal Each word mounts whole, as its own span after the text already shown, so earlier words never re-animate, re-wrap or move. A new word goes from opacity 0, blur(4px) and a 2px drop to rest over --dur-stream-word on --ease-out. No caret, no blink, no shimmer. When the answer ends the spans unwrap to plain text, and the message drops aria-busy so screen readers read it once, whole.

Pacing rate = max(30 characters a second, backlog / lag), where lag follows the gaps between chunks and stays between 300 and 400ms (--dur-stream-lag). The rate eases toward that target each frame, so speed changes are smooth. At the end the remainder flushes in about 60% of the lag.

Reduced motion --dur-stream-word is 0 and the keyframes are opacity only, so words appear at once with no blur or rise. The pacing stays, because it is reading speed, not decoration.

Suggestions and the request chips

Above results, chips repeat what Errand understood, one fact each. A chip filled from memory has the dashed inferred edge, so a guess never looks like something the person said. Under an answer, FollowUps offer at most three next requests; a press sends it as written and the row goes away.

Built from Chip in a named group, FollowUps and Message. The dashed edge is --errand-inferred-edge, always with the words "from memory" for screen readers and a line of explanation.

Motion A chip's wash and edge change over --dur-instant and it scales on press. Sent follow-ups leave on --ease-in; the bubble arrives on --ease-out.

Provider choice

Up to three providers as List rows: name, one tag, rating, earliest slot and price. Pressing a row picks it with the lime wash; the one lime button sends "I'll take" back to the agent, which holds the slot. A row never books.

Built from List with Avatar initials, Badge (accent only on Best match), formatAmount in the trailing slot, Skeleton, Empty and Button. Details opens a collapsing panel under the list.

Motion Results replace the still skeleton with a cross-fade, rows 40ms apart (half of --dur-instant, so reduced motion zeroes it too). The wash moves over --dur-instant. Details opens its height over --dur-base.

Service choice

When one request covers different jobs, Errand asks which, with a photo, a one-line description and the starting price for each. One option is chosen at a time and the lime button names it.

Built from Message, a radio group of Card options with a 4:3 photo flush to the top edge (clipped by the card's --radius-panel), Amount text and Button. Arrow keys move the choice. The selected card takes the lime wash and an olive edge with a check, as Chip does.

Motion Options arrive 40ms apart with a 4px rise, photos fade in over --dur-base, and the wash changes over --dur-instant.

Clarifying ask form

When a request misses a detail, Errand asks for only the missing fields, prefilled from memory. Each field has a label above it. Skip says what Errand will assume, so the agent never blocks.

Built from Field with FieldLabel, FieldDescription and FieldError, Input, Chip groups for the day and time, and Button. Send answers is the lime, size lg and full width.

Motion An error line opens under its field over --dur-base and focus moves to the field. On send, the form collapses and the answer arrives as the person's bubble.

Approval

Before Errand books, moves or cancels anything, it shows a fixed ApprovalRequest with every material detail, and only the person's press counts. Approve is the one glowing primary. If the price, slot or provider changes before the press, the card goes stale and a fresh card asks again.

Built from ApprovalRequest (Card, Amount, Button, Status), with the details as its children and three sourced reasons behind Why?. The pencil is named "Other options". Decline is the quiet ghost, never red. Approve is used only here; Confirm is kept for inferred facts.

Words Booking: "Approve ₹899". Reschedule: "Approve move". Cancellation: "Approve free cancellation" inside the free window, otherwise the fee, "Approve ₹200 fee".

Motion Approve scales to 0.98 on press, then its spinner fades in over --dur-fast while the label keeps its place. The outcome cross-fades into the footer at the same height, so nothing below moves, and takes focus. A stale card's warning line opens over --dur-base and the fresh card rises in.

After approval: checking with provider

After Approve, a Status says where the booking stands and ToolCall rows show each real step until the provider answers. Booked appears only with the provider's booking ID, on the solid booked mark.

Built from Status (running, then done or failed, inside role="status") and ToolCall rows in an aria-live region. Running steps are present tense, finished ones past tense, and a failed step says why. The booked mark is --errand-ok-fill with --errand-on-ok-fill.

Motion Each row arrives with a 4px rise over --dur-base. When a step finishes, its spinner gives way to the check at --scale-enter over --dur-fast and the label cross-fades to past tense.

Receipt

Once booked, the ActionReceipt records what Errand did, one past-tense line each with its amount. A booking cannot be undone, so the receipt says so and offers Reschedule and Cancel booking, which each lead to a fresh approval.

Built from ActionReceipt as an assistant Message, not a card. A failed line keeps its reason and the working lines stay. Each line has a hidden Done or Failed before it.

Motion Lines arrive 40ms apart with a 4px rise over --dur-base. A failed line opens in place.

Bookings, alerts and a provider cancellation

Upcoming bookings sit on home as a List. When a provider cancels, the row says so, an alert arrives, and the chat leads with what broke before proposing a replacement that names every difference and needs its own Approve. Errand never rebooks silently. On the day, the visit shows its status and the check-in code; after it, a rating.

Built from List with Status and ListIcon, Badge for the count, Message, ApprovalRequest with a list of differences, and for the visit, Status, Button and a star radio group.

Motion The booking's status cross-fades, the alert count changes with a 4px slide over --dur-fast, the alert row and the notice arrive on --ease-out, and the replacement card rises in. Stars fill over --dur-instant, with no pop.

Memory: inspect, correct and forget

A memory write shows as one quiet line, "Memory updated · View". View opens a sheet with every fact Errand keeps, where each can be corrected or forgotten. An inferred fact has the dashed edge and is not used until the person confirms it.

Built from Message, a link Button, Sheet with List rows and a row menu, Field and Input for a correction, and Toast. Done is the sheet's one lime. Confirm appears only on the inferred fact.

Motion The sheet rises over --dur-slow on --ease-out as the scrim fades in, and leaves faster on --ease-in. The row menu opens from --scale-enter over --dur-base. Correct opens a panel; Forget collapses the row and its tombstone line arrives.

Empty, error and loading

A region loads with still skeletons in the shape of its rows. If it fails, the error takes the region's place with Try again. If it loads with nothing, Empty says what belongs there and offers the one action that fills it.

Built from Skeleton (still, aria-hidden, with aria-busy and one hidden line on the region), ErrorState inline with a secondary Try again that shows its spinner, and Empty with one lime action.

Motion Each state cross-fades into the next over --dur-base. Nothing pulses or sweeps.

Toasts and dialogs

A toast confirms something the person just did, past tense, with at most one action. It is never the only record and never carries an error. A dialog holds a short task with a verb on its lime button.

Built from Toast (ink, one at a time, 5 seconds or 8 with an action, paused on hover or focus) and Dialog with Field and Input.

Motion A toast rises 8px over --dur-base and fades out over --dur-fast on --ease-in. The dialog opens from --scale-enter over --dur-slow while the scrim fades, and closes faster.

Operator console

The trace and the context log exist only here, for operators reviewing a session. The person's chat never shows a trace rail, raw tool names or what was left out of the model's context.

Built from a header with a Chip toggle, Message and ToolCall in the transcript, and a rail with trace rows (mono tool names, which are code) and a context list with Badges.

Motion The rail is a collapsing panel, so its width opens over --dur-base and the transcript reflows. Trace rows arrive 40ms apart and their bars grow from the start, and the running row's spinner gives way to its check.
